# ABHAYA - AI-Powered Mental Health & Support Coordination System
**Smart India Hackathon (SIH26094)**

ABHAYA is a confidential, multi-stakeholder support ecosystem designed for victims of atrocities. It bridges the critical gap between law enforcement, social care workers, and victims through proactive distress monitoring, explainable AI, and multi-tier assistance.

---

## 🏛️ Architecture Overview

The monorepo is structured across three core modules:

```text
ABHAYA/
├── backend/          # FastAPI REST API, SQLAlchemy DB, Multimodal AI & Acoustic Voice Engine
├── mobile-app/       # Flutter Victim App (Check-ins, SOS, Case Timeline, Support Requests)
└── web-dashboards/   # React Portals (Police Officer Onboarding & Social Worker Triage)
